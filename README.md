## Professional profile

I work at the intersection of **people development, communication, learning design and technology**. My focus is turning organizational needs into clear, practical and engaging digital experiences — from learning journeys and internal tools to interactive campaigns and rapid prototypes.

I currently work in **Learning, Development & Communications at AAK Colombia**, where I combine behavioral insight, instructional design, communication and AI-assisted development to build solutions for employees and internal stakeholders.

My background in **Psychology** helps me approach digital products from the human side first: behavior, adoption, clarity, motivation and experience.
